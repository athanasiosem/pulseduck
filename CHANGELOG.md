# Changelog

## 1.1

- Discard audio hits during holdoff instead of triggering them late.
- Align the first processing block when entering Tempo Sync.
- Honor the full curve duration in audio and MIDI trigger modes.
- Cap synchronized duration at 100% to preserve the selected beat interval.
- Return shortened custom curves to unity after completion.
- Update the playback marker for duration scaling.

## 1.0

- Initial rhythmic volume shaper with preset and editable curves.
- Add tempo, audio and MIDI modes, smoothing and low-band processing.
